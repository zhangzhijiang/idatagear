Part 1 — Privacy Policy

Who we are

McRecorder records audio and transcribes it into text using a speech model that runs on your own device. Recordings and transcripts are files in the app's private storage. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android.

McRecorder is published by iDataGear Inc, a company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Where this policy says we or us, it means iDataGear Inc.

There is no account to create and no sign-up. We do not operate a server that receives your content, and we hold no database in which you have a record.

What the app keeps on your device

The app writes the following to private storage on your device — an area other apps cannot read. It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

What Why Where it is kept
Audio recordings The recordings you made, as files. Your device only
Transcripts Text produced from your audio on the device. Your device only
The speech model The downloaded model file that makes offline transcription work. Your device only
Settings Language, theme, audio and display preferences. Your device only

None of this is uploaded to us. We cannot read it, cannot recover it for you, and are not able to hand it to anyone else, because it never reaches us.

Speech is transcribed on your device by a bundled model. Your audio is not uploaded, is not sent to a cloud transcription service, and is not used to train anything.

What leaves your device

This is the complete list. Everything not named here stays on your device.

What is sent Who receives it Purpose
A one-time model download GitHub, or Hugging Face as a fallback To fetch the offline speech model. Nothing about you is sent.

The first time you use offline transcription the app downloads the speech-recognition model — roughly 228 MB — from our releases page on GitHub, falling back to Hugging Face if that is unavailable. This is a one-way download of a file: nothing about you, and none of your audio, is sent in the request. Once the model is on your device, transcription runs entirely on the device and works with no network connection at all.

Separately from the list above, anything you deliberately send — through the system share sheet, an export, a printout or the clipboard — goes wherever you send it. That is your choice each time, and it is not covered by the table above.
