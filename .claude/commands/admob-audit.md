## Audit checklist (AdMob-specific)

**A. Test ads & test devices (prevents self-inflicted invalid traffic)**
- Live ad unit IDs must NOT serve in debug/development builds. Gate them on
  `BuildConfig.DEBUG` / flavor / `kDebugMode`, or use Google's official **test
  ad unit IDs** in debug (verify current IDs against Google's docs, e.g. banner
  `ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/6300978111`, interstitial `.../1033173712`,
  rewarded `.../5224354917`, app-open `.../9257395921`).
- Developer/test devices registered via
  `RequestConfiguration.Builder().setTestDeviceIds(...)`.
- No live ad IDs hard-coded where a test build can hit them.

**B. Ad placement / accidental clicks**
- >= ~50dp (ideally more) between any ad and interactive elements (buttons,
  list rows, nav bars, FABs, game controls). Flag overlaps, absolute
  positioning, or ads under a floating button.
- No ad placed where content shifts it under the user's finger during load.
- Banners not pinned flush against tappable UI; no ads on non-content screens.

**C. Interstitial / rewarded / app-open triggers & frequency**
- Interstitials NOT shown on app launch, mid-action, or on every screen
  transition; enforce a sensible frequency cap / minimum interval and natural
  break points only.
- Rewarded ads are strictly user-initiated (explicit opt-in), with the reward
  granted only via `onUserEarnedReward`.
- App-open ads not shown during the cold-start splash or over loading; respect
  cooldowns; don't stack with interstitials.
- Full-screen ads have a working dismiss and correct callbacks
  (`onAdDismissedFullScreenContent`, `onAdFailedToShowFullScreenContent`);
  reload happens after dismissal, not in a tight loop.

**D. Ad requests & refresh**
- Remove custom `Handler`/`Timer`/coroutine/`setInterval` loops that force-refresh
  ads. Let AdMob handle refresh (banner refresh is configured server-side).
- No duplicate/concurrent loads of the same ad unit; guard against re-requesting
  while a load or show is in flight, and across config changes / recompositions /
  widget rebuilds.

**E. Consent & privacy (UMP)**
- Google UMP / consent flow present and gating ad load where required (GDPR/EEA);
  iOS ATT if applicable. Serving personalized ads without consent can be treated
  as invalid.
- `app-ads.txt` exists, is published at the developer website in the Play listing,
  and contains the correct publisher line for this account.

**F. Logging & monitoring**
- Add/verify analytics hooks on the real SDK callbacks that exist: `onAdClicked`,
  `onAdImpression`, `onAdFailedToLoad`, `onPaidEvent` — log to
  Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics so CTR and anomalies are observable. (Only add
  callbacks the SDK actually provides.)
