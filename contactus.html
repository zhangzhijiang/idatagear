What to include in a support email

A problem we can reproduce gets fixed. Please tell us:

  • The app name, and whether you use it on iPhone or iPad, Android, or the web.
  • The app version, from the app's settings or profile screen.
  • Your device and OS version, for example “iPhone 15, iOS 26” or “Pixel 7, Android 15”.
  • The screen it happened on, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it is something you can see.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. We never need them.
