What McCards stores, and where

McCards has no sign-up and no login. It never asks for your name, email address or phone number. Almost everything you put into the app is stored only on your phone. A small anonymous record is kept on our server so the AI features can work.

DataWhere it is keptHow it is deleted
Your cards, coupons, shopping lists, receipts, photos and settings Only on your phone. They are never uploaded or synced to the cloud. By you, in the app or by uninstalling it (see below)
Anonymous account: a random Firebase Authentication ID for your installation, with no name, email, phone number or password Our server (Google Firebase) On request (see below)
AI usage record: your AI credit balance, a request counter used for rate limiting, and when the record was created and last changed Our server (Google Cloud Firestore) On request
Subscription record, only if you have subscribed: the Google Play purchase token, plan, status, expiry date and auto-renew setting Our server (Google Cloud Firestore) On request
Server error logs: your anonymous ID, recorded when an AI request fails or is rate limited. The text you sent to the AI feature is never logged. Google Cloud Logging Automatically after 30 days

How to request deletion of your account and data

  1. If you subscribe, cancel the subscription first. Deleting your data does not stop Google Play from billing you. In the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → McCards → Cancel subscription.
  2. Email us your deletion request. Write to idatagear@gmail.com with the subject line McCards data deletion request, and include:
    • a sentence saying you want your McCards account and data deleted;
    • if you have ever subscribed, your Google Play order number. It starts with GPA. and appears in the receipt email from Google Play and in your Google Play order history. This is how we find the records linked to your subscription.
    Email a deletion request

    We will never ask for your password, card numbers, PINs or photos.

  3. We process your request within 7 business days. We delete the records listed under What gets deleted and reply by email to confirm once it is done.
  4. Delete the data on your phone. We cannot do this step for you, because your cards, lists and receipts never leave your device. See the next section.

If you have never subscribed: your server record is tied only to a random ID for one installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account, so without an order number we cannot tell which record is yours. Uninstalling McCards deletes that ID from your phone. After that, no one, including us, can connect the record to you, and it holds nothing but a credit count, a request counter and two dates.

Deleting the data on your phone

  • One item at a time: delete any card, coupon, list or receipt in the app. Its photos are deleted with it.
  • Everything at once: uninstall McCards. This removes all of its data from the phone.
  • Want to keep a copy first? Use Settings → Back up everything before you uninstall. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

What gets deleted

When we process your request, we permanently delete:

  • your anonymous Firebase Authentication account;
  • your AI usage record, including your remaining AI credits;
  • your subscription record and the link between your Google Play purchase and your anonymous ID, if you have subscribed.

McCards does not keep your preferences, saved lists, cards, coupons or receipts in the cloud, so there is no server copy of them to delete. They exist only on your phone until you delete them there.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you install McCards again later, it starts a new, unrelated anonymous record.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Server error logs that contain your anonymous ID are deleted automatically 30 days after they are written. We do not delete them early.
  • Google Play's purchase records, such as your order history and payment details, are held by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details.

We keep nothing else.
