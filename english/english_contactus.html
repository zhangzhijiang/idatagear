Contact McEnglish

We read every message. Before you write, it is worth checking the three sections below — between them they account for most of the mail we get, and each takes about a minute to fix.

Try this first

There is no sound, or the wrong accent

The app has no built-in recordings; it speaks through the English text-to-speech voice installed on your device. If nothing plays, that voice is missing.

English voices are installed by default on nearly every device. If a translation is not being spoken, install a voice for that language (Chinese, Spanish or Arabic) in your device's text-to-speech settings.

Also check your media volume rather than your ringer volume — they are separate on most phones, and a silenced ringer does not mute text-to-speech. There is a step-by-step version of this in the Audio & voices section of the help page.

My purchase is missing on this device

Open Settings → Restore. The app also re-checks your purchase with the store every time it launches, so the usual cause is being signed in to a different account from the one you bought it on: your Apple Account on iPhone or iPad, your Google account on Android. Check the account, then reopen the app.

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Google Play under Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

My progress disappeared

ALL English stores everything on your device only. There is no account and no cloud backup, so uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or moving to a new phone will start you from scratch. This is a deliberate privacy trade-off — we hold no data about you — but it does mean lost progress cannot be restored. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

What to include

A bug we can reproduce gets fixed; one we cannot usually does not. Please tell us:

  • The app name — ALL English — and whether you are using the iPhone, iPad or Android app, or the web version.
  • The version and build number, from Settings → App Information.
  • Your device and OS version (for example "Pixel 7, Android 15").
  • Where in the app it happened — the screen name is enough.
  • What you expected, and what actually happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

We are a small team and reply in English. Please do not send passwords, payment details or any other sensitive information — we never need them, and we have no account system to use them with.

Reporting a content mistake

Corrections are genuinely welcome and we act on them. If a translation, pronunciation or example looks wrong, send us the exact entry — the word or sentence itself plus the screen you found it on — and what you think it should say. That is far more useful than a general note that a section has errors, and it lets us fix the underlying data rather than guess.
