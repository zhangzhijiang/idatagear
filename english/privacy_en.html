Privacy Policy

ALL English · Last updated: 16 August 2026

ALL English does not collect your personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Everything it needs ships inside the app, and everything you do stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. There is no sign-up, no login and no profile.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally, in its own private storage:

  • Your learning progress, test results and streak
  • Words and sentences you mark as favourites
  • Your settings: interface language, voice, accent and speech speed

This never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We cannot see it, recover it or transfer it between your devices.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking Practice asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. It is requested only when you first use that screen, and the rest of the app works without it.

When you hold the microphone button, your speech is passed to the speech recognition provided by your operating system - Apple on iOS, Google on Android - which returns text. We receive only that text, we compare it with the sentence on screen to produce a score, and we discard it. No audio is recorded, stored or transmitted by us.

Your operating system's speech recognition may process audio on the device or on its provider's servers, depending on your device, language and settings. That processing is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies, not by this one.

Purchases

Where the app offers an optional subscription, the purchase is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising. There is no advertising SDK, no Advertising ID collection and no data shared with any ad network.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. You are in full control: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
