Who we are

Xie Zi teaches Chinese handwriting: you trace each character in the correct stroke order and the app tells you where the stroke went wrong. Its 9,574-character dictionary and every stroke animation are bundled in the app, so practice works with no connection at all.

Xie Zi is published by iDataGear Inc, a company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Where this policy says we or us, it means iDataGear Inc.

There is no account to create and no sign-up. We do not operate a server that receives your content, and we hold no database in which you have a record.
