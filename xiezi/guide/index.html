Screen by screen

A guided tour of the app

The screenshots show the Simplified Chinese interface; the labels are given here in Chinese with an English gloss.

Home

The writing pad

The character fills the screen. Along the top: the speaker to hear it, ⓘ for its details, the 繁 chip showing its Traditional form, the character box with its pinyin, and search.

Along the bottom: 演示 Demo, 练习 Practice and 重置 Reset.

演示 Demo

Watch the stroke order

Tap 演示 and the character draws itself in the right order, one stroke at a time. Replay it as often as you like before you try.

ⓘ Details

Character details

汉字详情 shows the Traditional counterpart (寫 for 写), the 字形拆解 decomposition into components, and the 英文释义 English meaning.

练习 Practice

Write it yourself

Tap 练习 and trace the character over its outline. A correct stroke is accepted and stays; a wrong one is rejected so you can try again. 重置 clears the pad.

我的收藏 Favourites

Favourites and history

Star a character to keep it in 我的收藏. 我的记录 remembers what you practised. Both are kept on your device, and each has a button to clear it.

按HSK级别选择

Study by HSK level

Pick HSK1 to HSK6, HSK789 or RADICAL to work through a list, or tap 随机 for a random character to practise.

Tutorials

Three things worth doing first

Write your first character

  1. Tap search and type a character, or its pinyin.
  2. Tap 演示 Demo and watch the order once or twice.
  3. Tap 练习 Practice and trace it over the outline.
  4. If a stroke is rejected, check the direction and try it again.
  5. Tap the star to save it to your favourites.

Work through an HSK level

  1. Open 按HSK级别选择.
  2. Choose the level you are studying, from HSK1 to HSK789.
  3. Practise each character, then use 随机 to test yourself at random.

See the Traditional form

  1. Look at the 繁 chip at the top: it shows the Traditional counterpart.
  2. Tap ⓘ to see both forms with the decomposition.
  3. Practise the Traditional form the same way.
FAQ

Common questions

How many characters does Xie Zi have?

More than 9,500 characters and radicals, each with pinyin and an English meaning, grouped by HSK 1–6, HSK 7–9 and radicals.

Does it teach Simplified or Traditional characters?

Both. Simplified and Traditional counterparts are shown side by side, and you can practise either.

Is the stroke order correct?

The app uses authentic stroke data, so the order is the one you would be taught.

Does it work offline?

Stroke data is cached on your device, so a character you have traced once works with no connection at all. A character you have never opened needs a connection the first time.

Do I need an account?

No account and no sign-up. Your favourites, history and progress stay on your device.

Can I hear how a character is pronounced?

Yes. Tap the speaker to hear any character read aloud in Mandarin.

Is there a web version?

Yes: open Xie Zi in your browser. It also runs on phones and tablets from the App Store and Google Play.
