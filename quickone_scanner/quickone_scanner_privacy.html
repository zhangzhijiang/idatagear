QuickOne Scanner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

QuickOne Scanner is an Android document scanner. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information. It is written to be accurate rather than reassuring; where a third party is involved, it says so.

The short version. Your scanned documents never leave your device. QuickOne Scanner has no account system, no cloud sync, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not operate a server, and we never receive your documents, your images, or your recognized text.

1. Information we collect

None. The developer of QuickOne Scanner collects no personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, no account, and no user profile. We do not use any analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK. We have no server that receives data from the app.

2. Your documents and scanned images

Scans, page images, recognized text, folder names, and document titles are stored only in the app's private storage area on your device. Other apps cannot read them. They are never uploaded anywhere.

All processing — edge detection, image enhancement, filters, and text recognition (OCR) — happens locally on your device.

When you use the Share or Export features, you choose where a copy goes. Once you hand a file to another app (email, cloud storage, messaging), that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

3. Why the app needs internet access

QuickOne Scanner requests the INTERNET permission. It is used for two purposes only, neither of which involves your documents:

  • Purchases. Google Play Billing requires network access to process and restore the one-time purchase.
  • One-time component download. The document scanner and the text-recognition model are delivered through Google Play services and download on first use. After that first download, scanning and text recognition work fully offline.

4. Third-party services

QuickOne Scanner uses components provided by Google. We do not control what these components send to Google, and their use of any data is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

ComponentPurposeNotes
Google Play services (ML Kit Document Scanner) The camera capture, edge detection, and page cleanup interface Runs inside Google Play services. Your captured pages are returned to QuickOne Scanner and stored on your device.
Google ML Kit Text Recognition On-device OCR (Latin script) The recognition model runs locally after its one-time download.
Google Play Billing The one-time purchase and its restoration Google processes the payment. We never see or store your payment details.

For transparency: Google's ML Kit and Play services libraries include Google's own diagnostic and usage-reporting components, and they add the ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission to the app. Any such reporting is Google's, is described in Google's Privacy Policy, and is separate from the developer of QuickOne Scanner, who receives nothing. The contents of your documents are not part of it.

QuickOne Scanner does not request camera permission — capture happens inside Google Play services, which uses its own. It does not request storage permissions; scans are kept in app-private storage.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. Deleting a document in the app removes its images and recognized text from your device. Uninstalling QuickOne Scanner removes everything the app stored.

There is no cloud backup. Because nothing is uploaded, a lost, reset, or broken phone means the scans on it are gone. Use Settings → Export library as ZIP to keep your own copy somewhere safe.

7. Children

QuickOne Scanner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
