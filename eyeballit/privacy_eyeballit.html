Privacy Policy

None. The Game has no user accounts, no login, and no sign-up. We do not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email address, location, or any other personal information to our servers. The Game does not include any first-party analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your own device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of data locally in your browser (using local storage) — for example your current level, best streak, best accuracy, and your chosen theme, language, and volume settings. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.
