Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about McLearn® apps, downloads, and iDataGear services.

How do I learn Mandarin Chinese with HSK preparation?

Learn Chinese by McLearn® covers HSK 1–6 with vocabulary, reading comprehension, and pronunciation practice. Open the web app for free or download on Google Play. No account required. Free on web and Android.

How do I learn Cantonese (廣東話)?

McCantonese — Learn Cantonese carries 12,000+ words with Jyutping on every entry, 3,100+ phrases, 1,400+ everyday sentences, 200+ dialogues and 270+ mini stories, plus Hong Kong slang, Gen Z slang and Guangzhou expressions. A full Jyutping guide covers the 19 initials, the finals and the six tones, and speaking practice uses speech recognition so you can check yourself. Get it on the App Store, Google Play, or use the web app. For lookups, pair it with the Great Cantonese Dictionary.

How do I use the Great Cantonese Dictionary?

Install it from the App Store or Google Play, or open idatagear.com/gcd/ in any browser. Search 71,000+ entries three ways — by Chinese characters (你好), by Jyutping (nei5 hou2), or by English meaning. Every entry gives the characters, the Jyutping and clear definitions; tap the speaker to hear it. The whole dictionary is built in, so it works entirely offline. The dictionary itself is free forever; an optional Premium subscription (US$2.99/month, first month free, or US$12.99 for 6 months) unlocks seven curated collections and removes ads.

How do I learn English with CEFR levels?

ALL English (All Level Learner's English) by McLearn® is CEFR-aligned (A1–C2) with vocabulary with IPA, sentences, articles, practice tests, and pronunciation practice. Use the web app or download on Google Play. Free on both platforms.

How do I practice Chinese character stroke order?

Use Chinese Xie Zi (中文寫字板) — on the App Store, Google Play, or the web app. You trace each character on screen and get real-time stroke feedback, and you can replay the animated stroke order as often as you need. It covers 9,500+ characters and radicals with pinyin and English meanings, character decomposition, and Simplified/Traditional counterparts, grouped by HSK 1–6, HSK 7–9 and radicals. Free to download, ad-supported, with an optional one-time US$1.99 Remove Ads purchase.

What is Jyutping romanization for Cantonese?

Jyutping is the standard Cantonese romanization system developed by the Linguistic Society of Hong Kong. It uses Latin letters and tone numbers 1–6 to represent Cantonese pronunciation (e.g., 你好 = nei5 hou2). McLearn's Learn Cantonese and Great Cantonese Dictionary include full Jyutping for every word, with audio. Both are free.

What is Eyeball It and how do you play it?

Eyeball It! is a free casual visual-estimation puzzle game with three modes: Length & Shapes (drag a line to match a shape's perimeter by eye), Liquid Volume (hold to pour and release at the target volume), and Color Volume (mix cyan, magenta, and yellow to match a colour). Every round ends with an animated accuracy reveal. Play free on the web or get it on Google Play.

What is Momera.ME and is my journal data private?

Momera.ME is a free local-first moments journal for Android. What is QuickOne Scanner and how does it scan documents?

QuickOne Scanner: PDF & OCR is a free document scanner for iPhone, iPad and Android. Hold the camera over a page and it finds the edges for you; capture several pages in one session, reorder them by dragging, crop, and apply Original, Grayscale or Black & white filters — always reversible, because the untouched original is never overwritten.

On-device text recognition reads Latin scripts and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), so you can search full text across every word in your pages, copy the text out, or export a PDF with an invisible searchable text layer. You can also sign a page with your finger, add sticky notes, and export as PDF, JPEG, .txt or a ZIP of your whole library.

Download on the App Store or Google Play, or read how it works.

Is QuickOne Scanner really free, and does it upload my documents?

Yes, genuinely free — no subscription, no one-time unlock, no scan allowance, no ads and no watermark on any export. And nothing is uploaded: edge detection, image cleanup, text recognition and search all run locally. Your scans, page images and recognized text stay in the app's private storage. We operate no server for this app and receive nothing. There is no account, no email and no sign-in.

The camera is used only to scan a page, and photo import goes through the system picker, which needs no photo-library access. On iPhone and iPad the capture screen is Apple's own VisionKit document camera, and both text-recognition models ship inside the app — so OCR works offline from the very first scan.

The trade-off worth knowing: because nothing is uploaded, your scans exist only on that device. Use Settings → Export library as ZIP to keep your own copy. Full privacy policy.

What is the best offline Cantonese dictionary for iPhone?

The Great Cantonese Dictionary by McLearn® is a fully offline Cantonese reference for iPhone and iPad, free on the App Store. It holds over 71,000 words, phrases and expressions, each with Jyutping romanization and spoken pronunciation, and the entire dictionary is built into the app — nothing to download and no connection needed, so it works on the MTR, on a plane, or anywhere your data does not reach.

Search three ways: by Chinese characters, by Jyutping when you know the sound but not the character, or by English meaning. It also covers what a dictionary usually leaves out — popular everyday words, Hong Kong Gen Z slang, Guangzhou slang, AABB and ABB reduplication, tongue twisters, children's songs and the Three Character Classic. Interface in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Requires iOS 15 or later.

How much do McCantonese Premium and Great Cantonese Premium cost?

Does McCantonese work offline and can I practise speaking?

Yes to both. The whole library is stored on your device, so browsing and audio keep working on a plane or a train, and there is no account and no sign-up. For speaking, the app has a speaking-practice screen with speech recognition so you can check yourself, plus tappable audio on every word, sentence and story, an adjustable speech rate, and a choice of installed Cantonese voices.
