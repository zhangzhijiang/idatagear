FAQ

Questions people ask us.

What apps does iDataGear make?

Eight core apps: McChinese for Mandarin and HSK, McCantonese for spoken Cantonese, McEnglish (ALL English on Google Play) for CEFR A1–C2 English, the Great Cantonese Dictionary with 71,655 entries, Xie Zi for writing characters stroke by stroke, QuickOne Scanner for on-device PDF scanning and OCR, Momera GO for hikes and journeys, and McMuse, which turns a photo into art in 51 styles.

Which iDataGear apps are on iPhone and iPad?

McChinese, McCantonese, McEnglish, the Great Cantonese Dictionary, Chinese Xie Zi, QuickOne Scanner and Momera GO are on the App Store, and all of them are on Google Play too. McMuse is on Google Play for Android.

Does the Great Cantonese Dictionary work offline?

Yes. All 71,655 entries, with Jyutping, are built into the app, so search works with no connection. Search by Chinese characters, by Jyutping, by English meaning or by Mandarin.

How can I learn to read Jyutping?

In McCantonese, open Speaking → Jyutping. The guide covers the 19 initials, the finals and the six tones with audio, and Jyutping Practice then breaks real characters into initial, final and tone. The ten-minute walkthrough shows the order to do it in.

Which app helps me prepare for the HSK exam?

McChinese covers HSK 1 to 6 (500, 772, 974, 1,000, 1,071 and 1,149 words), plus 1,530 elementary-school words, 1,988 example sentences, 33 graded articles and 309 reading test questions, with pinyin and audio throughout.

How do I practise writing Chinese characters?

Chinese Xie Zi lets you trace any of 9,500+ characters and radicals with real-time stroke feedback: correct strokes are accepted, wrong ones are rejected. It plays the animated stroke order, breaks characters into components, and groups them by HSK 1–6, HSK 7–9 and radicals.

Which app teaches English by CEFR level?

McEnglish, listed as ALL English on Google Play, organises over 8,000 words with IPA from A1 to C2, adds collections such as Oxford 3000, IELTS, CET-4 and CET-6, and includes timed level tests, speaking practice and two hands-free listening modes.

Does QuickOne Scanner upload my documents?

No. Edge detection, image cleanup, text recognition and search all run on the device, and scans stay in the app's private storage. There is no account, and we run no server for the app.

How does McMuse turn a photo into art?

Pick a photo and one or more of 51 styles, then choose 1K, 2K or 4K. McMuse strips location and other metadata from the photo and sends it to Google's Gemini to repaint it, after asking you first. The artwork comes straight back to your phone and stays in the gallery there.

Can Momera GO record a hike with no signal?

Yes. GPS recording never needs a connection, and map areas you have looked at before are kept on the phone so they still draw offline. Photos, videos, voice memos and notes are pinned to the route as you walk.
