Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about McLearn® apps, downloads, and iDataGear services.

How do I learn Mandarin Chinese with HSK preparation?

Learn Chinese by McLearn® covers HSK 1–3 with vocabulary, reading comprehension, and pronunciation practice. Open the web app for free or download on Google Play. No account required. Free on web and Android.

How do I learn Cantonese (廣東話)?

Learn Cantonese features story-driven lessons, Hong Kong slang, Jyutping romanization, and audio-rich practice. Open the web app or download on Google Play. Also available: the Great Cantonese Dictionary for vocabulary lookups.

How do I use the Great Cantonese Dictionary?

Open idatagear.com/gcd/ in your browser or install the Android app. Search any Cantonese word to get Jyutping romanization, tone data, idioms, and cultural usage notes. Bookmark favourites for study. Works offline. Completely free.

How do I learn English with CEFR levels?

ALL English (All Level Learner's English) by McLearn® is CEFR-aligned (A1–C2) with vocabulary with IPA, sentences, articles, practice tests, and pronunciation practice. Use the web app or download on Google Play. Free on both platforms.

How do I practise Chinese character stroke order?

Use Xie Zi (写字板) at idatagear.com/xiezi/ or the Android app. It provides interactive stroke order practice powered by HanziWriter with real-time visual feedback and green checkmarks for correct strokes. Covers HSK character sets. Free.

Where can I download all McLearn apps?

All McLearn® apps by iDataGear Inc are free on Google Play: Learn Chinese, Learn Cantonese, ALL English, Great Cantonese Dictionary, and Xie Zi. All apps also run on the web — no download required.

Does iDataGear offer BI consulting or custom software development?

Yes. iDataGear Inc provides ETL data processing, Business Intelligence implementations, analytics dashboards, workflow automation, and end-to-end product development for web and mobile. Contact us to discuss your project.

What is Jyutping romanization for Cantonese?

Jyutping is the standard Cantonese romanization system developed by the Linguistic Society of Hong Kong. It uses Latin letters and tone numbers 1–6 to represent Cantonese pronunciation (e.g., 你好 = nei5 hou2). McLearn's Learn Cantonese and Great Cantonese Dictionary include full Jyutping for every word, with audio. Both are free.

Is iDataGear free to use?

All McLearn® consumer apps are completely free — no subscriptions, paywalls, or required sign-ups. Learn Chinese, Learn Cantonese, ALL English, Xie Zi, Great Cantonese Dictionary, and Open Interview are free on the web at idatagear.com and on Android via Google Play — as are the Eyeball It! game and the Momera.ME and Momera.Hiking utilities. iDataGear's BI consulting and custom software services are paid professional engagements — contact us for a quote.

What is Eyeball It! and how do you play it?

Eyeball It! is a free casual visual-estimation puzzle game with three modes: Length & Shapes (drag a line to match a shape's perimeter by eye), Liquid Volume (hold to pour and release at the target volume), and Color Volume (mix cyan, magenta, and yellow to match a colour). Every round ends with an animated accuracy reveal. Play free on the web or get it on Google Play.

What is Momera.ME and is my journal data private?

Momera.ME is a free local-first moments journal for Android. Capture life by voice, text, and photo — speech-to-text runs entirely on your device, so nothing is uploaded: no cloud, no account, fully offline. Photos can be scanned with on-device text recognition. Get it on Google Play — see the privacy policy.

What is Momera.Hiking and does it work offline?

Momera.Hiking is a free offline-first GPS hike tracker. It records routes with background GPS, works on trails with no signal thanks to offline OpenStreetMap maps, lets you drop geo-tagged photo, video, and voice waypoints, keeps a hike calendar with history, and exports animated track replays as video. Try the web version or get it on Google Play.
