Privacy Policy

Momera GO

The short version

  • The app has no account and no sign-up.
  • We do not sell any personal data, and none of your hikes, journeys or media is ever transmitted anywhere.
  • Your hikes, GPS tracks, photos, videos, voice memos, and notes are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • The app does send anonymous crash and diagnostic reports (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics) so faults can be found and fixed. These contain no hike data, no location, no media and nothing that identifies you. See Crash and diagnostic reporting.

Network connections

The app works offline for tracking. It makes network requests only to:

  • Map tiles — map imagery is fetched from OpenStreetMap or a tile source you configure in Settings. The map area you view is requested from that provider, as with any map app. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports — sent to Google Firebase. See below.

We do not operate a backend server for this app. Apart from the crash and diagnostic reports described below, we receive no data from it.

Crash and diagnostic reporting

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics, both operated by Google, to report crashes and basic usage diagnostics. This is how we find out that something is broken for real users.

  • What is sent: crash stack traces, the app version, the device model and OS version, and which screens were opened.
  • What is never sent: your GPS tracks, hikes, journeys, photos, videos, voice memos or notes. None of your content leaves the device.
  • Advertising ID: the app explicitly removes the advertising-ID permissions that the Firebase library would otherwise add, so no advertising identifier is collected and none is available to the app.
  • Retention: governed by Google’s Firebase data retention. See the Firebase privacy documentation.

Reporting is active in released versions of the app and there is currently no in-app switch to turn it off. If you would rather not send these reports, do not install the app, or uninstall it — uninstalling stops all reporting.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell any personal data, and there are no advertising partners in the app. The only third party that receives anything is Google, as the processor of the crash and diagnostic reports described above.

Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. You can delete any hike (and its media) from within the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app or using Android’s Settings → Apps → Momera GO → Storage → Clear storage.

Permissions

The app requests: location (including background location), camera, microphone, physical activity (step counter), notifications, and internet access. Each is used only for the purposes described above. You can revoke any permission in Android Settings; some features will stop working without them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s listing.
