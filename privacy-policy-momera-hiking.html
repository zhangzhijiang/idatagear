The short version

  • Momera GO has no account and no sign-up. We do not know who you are.
  • Your hikes, GPS tracks, journeys, photos, videos, voice memos and notes are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and we cannot see them.
  • We run no server for this app. There is nowhere for your content to be sent.
  • Only two kinds of request leave the app: map tiles from OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap, and crash and diagnostic reports on Android.
  • We do not sell personal data, and the app contains no advertising SDK.

Who we are

Momera GO (also published as 拾光旅行) is made by iDataGear. This policy covers the Momera GO mobile app on Android and iOS, and nothing else. You can reach us at idatagear@gmail.com.

For the purposes of data protection law, iDataGear is the controller for the small amount of diagnostic data described below. For everything you create in the app, there is no controller other than you: that data never reaches us.

Importing your Google Maps Timeline

Momera GO can turn a Google Maps Timeline export into journeys you can replay. Because a Timeline export is a detailed history of where you have been, it is worth being exact about what happens to it:

  • You export the file from Google yourself and choose it with your device's file picker. The app never reaches into your Google account and has no access to it.
  • The file is read and parsed entirely on your device. It is never uploaded — not to us, not to anyone. The app has no server to upload it to.
  • Only the days you choose to import become journeys. The rest of the file is ignored and nothing is retained from it.
  • Each journey is written to your device as a GeoJSON file that belongs to you, and you can export or delete it at any time.

Building a journey from photos

You can also build a journey from geotagged photos. The app uses your operating system's own photo picker — PHPickerViewController on iOS, the Android photo picker on Android — which hands the app only the specific pictures you select.

  • Momera GO therefore does not request access to your photo library. On Android the READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO and READ_MEDIA_AUDIO permissions are explicitly removed from the app's manifest; on iOS no photo library permission is requested at all.
  • For the photos you pick, the app reads the capture time and coordinates stored inside the picture (its EXIF metadata) to place it on a map. This happens on your device.
  • On Android the app does request ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION. This exists only so the coordinates inside a picture you selected are not stripped out before the app can read them. Without it Android hands over the photo with its GPS tags zeroed, and a photo journey cannot be built.
  • Neither the pictures nor their metadata are uploaded anywhere.

What actually leaves your device

Two things, and nothing else.

1. Map tiles

The map is drawn from imagery fetched as you pan and zoom, from OpenStreetMap (Street View) or OpenTopoMap (Outdoor View), depending on the basemap you choose in Settings.

As with every map app, the tiles you request tell that provider roughly which part of the world you are looking at, together with your IP address. We do not operate those servers and we receive nothing from them. Their handling of those requests is covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and by OpenTopoMap respectively.

Tiles you have already viewed are cached on your device, so revisiting an area does not necessarily fetch anything. Recording a hike itself needs no connection at all — GPS works offline, and the only reason the app uses the network is to draw the map.

2. Crash and diagnostic reports

So that faults can be found and fixed, the Android version sends crash reports and a small number of diagnostic events using Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics, both operated by Google.

The iOS version currently sends none of this. The iOS build ships without Firebase configuration, so the reporting libraries never start and no crash or diagnostic data is sent from an iPhone at all. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships.

What is sent:

  • Crash and error reports: the stack trace, the app version, the device model and operating system version.
  • Which screens were opened, so we can tell where a crash happened.
  • A small set of diagnostic events about the health of the GPS recorder — for example that the location stream stopped and was reopened, how many attempts that took, how many seconds passed since the last fix, whether a hike was recovered after the app was killed, and whether adaptive GPS was on. These are counters, states and timings.
  • Firebase also generates a random, app-specific installation identifier and derives a coarse country or region from the IP address of the request, as it does for any app that uses it.

What is never sent:

  • Your coordinates. No latitude or longitude is ever included in a report or an event — the GPS diagnostics carry accuracy in metres, timings and counts, never a position.
  • Your hikes, journeys, trails, photos, videos, voice memos or notes.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts — the app never asks for any of them.
  • Any advertising identifier. The Android build explicitly removes the AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID permissions that the Firebase libraries would otherwise add, so no advertising ID is collected and none is even available to the app.

Google processes this data on our behalf; its retention is governed by Firebase's own data handling. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. There is currently no in-app switch to turn reporting off; if you would rather not send it, please do not install the app, or uninstall it — uninstalling ends it immediately.

What the app never does

  • No account, no sign-up, no login, no profile.
  • No advertising SDK is present, no ads are displayed, and no data is shared with any advertising network.
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing of personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising, under any privacy law — including the CCPA/CPRA, where we make no such sale or share.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites. The app presents no App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS because it does no tracking, and iOS accordingly gives it an all-zero advertising identifier.
  • No cloud sync, no social features, no contacts access, no calendar access, no health data.

Keeping and deleting data

Because your content only ever lives on your device, you are the one who controls how long it is kept. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

  • Delete an individual hike, journey, trail, photo, video, voice memo or note from inside the app.
  • Clear the cached map tiles from Settings → Storage.
  • Remove everything at once by uninstalling the app. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Momera GO → Storage → Clear storage.

Anything you deliberately exported — a GPX file, a GeoJSON journey, a video you saved to your gallery or shared — is an ordinary file outside the app, and deleting the app does not remove it. Those copies are yours to manage.

Crash and diagnostic reports already sent are held by Firebase under Google's retention periods. Uninstalling stops any further reports.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or erase personal data held about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority.

In practice, the app is built so that there is very little for these rights to apply to. We hold no account, no identifiers you gave us, and none of your hikes or media. The only data we have any relationship with is the pseudonymous crash and diagnostic reporting described above, which is not linked to your identity and which we cannot connect back to an individual person. If you would like to exercise a right, or you simply want to ask what we hold, write to idatagear@gmail.com and we will answer.

To erase everything the app itself holds, uninstall it — that is immediate and complete, and needs no request to us.

International transfers

Your own content does not move, because it never leaves your device. The crash and diagnostic reports are processed by Google on infrastructure that may be located outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards Google applies to Firebase.

Children

Momera GO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The app has no account system, no social features, no messaging and no user-generated content that anyone else can see. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will address it.

Security

Your recordings are held in the app's private storage, which the operating system keeps separate from other apps. Because nothing is uploaded, there is no server holding your hikes that could be breached. Please note that anyone with access to your unlocked device, or to an unencrypted backup of it, can reach the app's data — a device passcode and device encryption remain your best protection.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page changes with them and the “Last updated” date above will move. Where a change is significant — new data being collected, or anything being sent somewhere new — we will update this page before that version of the app ships.

导入 Google 地图时间轴

拾光旅行可以把 Google 地图时间轴的导出文件变成可以回放的行程。由于时间轴导出文件是一份详细的行踪历史，这里有必要把处理方式说清楚：

  • 文件由你自己从 Google 导出，再通过设备的文件选择器交给应用。应用不会接触你的 Google 账号，也没有任何访问权限。
  • 文件完全在你的设备上读取和解析。它从不上传——不会传给我们，也不会传给任何人。应用根本没有可以上传的服务器。
  • 只有你选择导入的那些日期会变成行程，文件里其余的内容会被忽略，不会留下任何痕迹。
  • 每段行程都会以 GeoJSON 文件写入你的设备，归你所有，你随时可以导出或删除。

用照片生成行程

你也可以用带定位的照片生成行程。应用使用的是系统自带的照片选择器——iOS 上是 PHPickerViewController，Android 上是系统照片选择器——它只会把你选中的那几张照片交给应用。

  • 因此拾光旅行不会申请访问你的相册。在 Android 上，READ_MEDIA_IMAGES、READ_MEDIA_VIDEO 和 READ_MEDIA_AUDIO 权限已从应用清单中明确移除；在 iOS 上则完全不申请任何相册权限。
  • 对于你选中的照片，应用会读取照片文件里本来就带有的拍摄时间和坐标（即 EXIF 元数据），用来把它放到地图上。这个过程在你的设备上完成。
  • 在 Android 上应用会申请 ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION。它的唯一用途，是让你选中的那张照片里的坐标不被系统抹掉。没有它，Android 交给应用的照片其 GPS 标签会被清零，照片行程也就无从生成。
  • 照片本身和它的元数据都不会被上传到任何地方。

究竟有什么会离开你的设备

只有两样，此外没有别的。

一、地图瓦片

地图是随着你的平移和缩放逐块加载出来的。根据你在设置里选择的底图，图像来自 OpenStreetMap（街道视图）或 OpenTopoMap（户外视图）。

和所有地图应用一样，你请求的瓦片会让对方大致知道你正在看世界上的哪一块区域，同时也会看到你的 IP 地址。这些服务器不由我们运营，我们也不会从中收到任何数据。它们如何处理这些请求，分别适用 OpenStreetMap 基金会隐私政策和 OpenTopoMap 的相关说明。

已经看过的瓦片会缓存在你的设备上，所以重新回到同一区域未必会产生新的请求。记录徒步本身完全不需要联网——GPS 在离线状态下照常工作，应用之所以使用网络，只是为了画出地图。

二、崩溃与诊断报告

为了能够发现并修复故障，Android 版本会通过 Google 提供的 Firebase Crashlytics 和 Firebase Analytics 发送崩溃报告和少量诊断事件。

iOS 版本目前完全不发送这些内容。iOS 构建没有包含 Firebase 配置，相关库根本不会启动，因此 iPhone 上不会发出任何崩溃或诊断数据。若今后有所改变，我们会在该版本发布之前更新本政策。

会发送的内容：

  • 崩溃和错误报告：调用堆栈、应用版本号、设备型号和操作系统版本。
  • 打开过哪些界面，以便判断崩溃发生在什么位置。
  • 一小组关于 GPS 记录器健康状况的诊断事件——例如定位数据流中断后被重新打开、重试了几次、距上一次定位过了多少秒、应用被系统杀掉后是否成功恢复了徒步记录、以及是否启用了自适应 GPS。这些都是计数、状态和时间。
  • 与使用 Firebase 的任何应用一样，Firebase 也会生成一个随机的、仅限本应用的安装标识符，并根据请求的 IP 地址推断出粗略的国家或地区。

绝不会发送的内容：

  • 你的坐标。任何报告或事件中都不包含经纬度——GPS 诊断携带的是以米为单位的精度、时间和次数，从来不是位置。
  • 你的徒步记录、行程、步道、照片、视频、语音备忘或笔记。
  • 你的姓名、邮箱、电话号码或通讯录——应用从来不索取这些。
  • 任何广告标识符。Android 构建已明确移除 Firebase 库本会添加的 AD_ID 和 ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID 权限，因此不会收集广告 ID，应用自身也根本拿不到。

Google 作为受托方代表我们处理这些数据，其保留期限适用 Firebase 自身的数据处理规则。我们的合法依据是维持应用正常运行这一正当利益。目前应用内没有关闭上报的开关；如果你不希望发送，请不要安装，或者卸载应用——卸载会立即终止上报。

应用绝不会做的事

  • 没有账号、不需要注册、没有登录、没有个人资料。
  • 不包含任何广告 SDK，不展示广告，也不会把任何数据分享给广告网络。
  • 不出售个人数据；在任何隐私法律下（包括美国加州 CCPA/CPRA）都不存在为跨场景行为广告而"出售"或"共享"个人数据的情形。

  • 没有云同步、没有社交功能、不访问通讯录、不访问日历、不涉及健康数据。

数据的保存与删除

由于你的内容始终只存在于你的设备上，保存多久完全由你决定。我们这边没有任何东西需要代你删除。

  • 在应用内删除单条徒步记录、行程、步道、照片、视频、语音备忘或笔记。
  • 在设置 → 存储空间中清除地图瓦片缓存。

已经发送出去的崩溃与诊断报告保存在 Firebase，适用 Google 的保留期限。卸载应用会终止后续的一切上报。

安全

究竟有什麼會離開你的裝置

只有兩樣，此外沒有別的。

一、地圖圖磚

地圖是隨著你的平移和縮放逐塊載入出來的。依你在設定裡選擇的底圖，影像來自 OpenStreetMap（街道檢視）或 OpenTopoMap（戶外檢視）。

和所有地圖 App 一樣，你請求的圖磚會讓對方大致知道你正在看世界上的哪一塊區域，同時也會看到你的 IP 位址。這些伺服器不由我們營運，我們也不會從中收到任何資料。它們如何處理這些請求，分別適用 OpenStreetMap 基金會隱私權政策和 OpenTopoMap 的相關說明。

已經看過的圖磚會快取在你的裝置上，所以重新回到同一區域未必會產生新的請求。記錄健行本身完全不需要連網——GPS 在離線狀態下照常運作，App 之所以使用網路，只是為了畫出地圖。

二、當機與診斷報告

為了能夠發現並修復故障，Android 版本會透過 Google 提供的 Firebase Crashlytics 和 Firebase Analytics 傳送當機報告和少量診斷事件。

iOS 版本目前完全不會傳送這些內容。iOS 建置沒有包含 Firebase 設定，相關函式庫根本不會啟動，因此 iPhone 上不會發出任何當機或診斷資料。若日後有所改變，我們會在該版本發布之前更新本政策。

會傳送的內容：

  • 當機和錯誤報告：呼叫堆疊、App 版本號、裝置型號和作業系統版本。
  • 開啟過哪些畫面，以便判斷當機發生在什麼位置。
  • 一小組關於 GPS 記錄器健康狀況的診斷事件——例如定位資料流中斷後被重新開啟、重試了幾次、距上一次定位過了多少秒、App 被系統終止後是否成功復原了健行記錄、以及是否啟用了自適應 GPS。這些都是計數、狀態和時間。
  • 與使用 Firebase 的任何 App 一樣，Firebase 也會產生一個隨機的、僅限本 App 的安裝識別碼，並根據請求的 IP 位址推斷出粗略的國家或地區。

絕不會傳送的內容：

  • 你的座標。任何報告或事件中都不包含經緯度——GPS 診斷攜帶的是以公尺為單位的精確度、時間和次數，從來不是位置。
  • 你的健行記錄、行程、步道、相片、影片、語音備忘或筆記。
  • 你的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼或通訊錄——App 從來不索取這些。
  • 任何廣告識別碼。Android 建置已明確移除 Firebase 函式庫本會加入的 AD_ID 和 ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID 權限，因此不會收集廣告 ID，App 自身也根本拿不到。

Google 作為受託方代表我們處理這些資料，其保留期限適用 Firebase 自身的資料處理規則。我們的合法依據是維持 App 正常運作這一正當利益。目前 App 內沒有關閉回報的開關；如果你不希望傳送，請不要安裝，或者解除安裝 App——解除安裝會立即終止回報。
