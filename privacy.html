Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Welcome to iDataGear Inc ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the website idatagear.com and provide language learning applications, business intelligence solutions, and related services. Some of our mobile applications may request location permissions. We only collect location data if you explicitly grant permission, and you can disable location services at any time through your device settings.
