Part 1 — Privacy Policy

Who we are

McMuse turns a photo you already have into a piece of art, in the art style you choose. The rendering is done by Google's Gemini image model; the artwork comes back to your phone and is kept there. It runs on Android.

McMuse is published by iDataGear Inc, a company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Where this policy says we or us, it means iDataGear Inc.

McMuse runs one service of its own: it receives the photo you choose, has it rendered, and returns the artwork. It writes no photo and no artwork to any disk of ours. You do not need an account to use the app, and signing in with Google is optional. What we keep on our side is described under What we hold on our side below.

The app writes the following to private storage on your device — an area other apps cannot read. It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Content safety and reporting

McMuse does not review your photos or your artworks: they are not kept on our side, and nobody at iDataGear sees them. The only screening is done by the safety filters of Google's Gemini service, which we set to block content at a medium level of concern or above in four categories — sexually explicit, hateful, harassing and dangerous content. A filter can refuse a photo, or the artwork made from it. When it does, no artwork is made and the credits it would have cost are returned to you.

No filter is perfect, and we cannot promise that it catches everything. If an artwork is offensive or should not have been made, report it from inside the app: open the artwork, choose Report, pick a reason — sexual content, violent or disturbing, hateful or abusive, or something else — and add a note if you wish. The report is sent without the picture, so it lets us reproduce how the artwork was made but does not show us the artwork itself.

What you may and may not do with the app is set out in the acceptable-use section of the terms below.
