Part 1 — Privacy Policy

Who we are

McMuse turns a photo you already have into a piece of art, in one of sixteen styles. The rendering is done by Google's Gemini image model; the artwork comes back to your phone and is kept there. It runs on Android.

McMuse is published by iDataGear Inc, a company incorporated in British Columbia, Canada. Where this policy says we or us, it means iDataGear Inc.

There is no account to create and no sign-up. McMuse does run one service of its own: it receives the photo you choose, has it rendered, and returns the artwork. It writes no photo and no artwork to any disk of ours. The only thing we keep is described under What we hold on our side below.

What the app keeps on your device

The app writes the following to private storage on your device — an area other apps cannot read. It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

What Why Where it is kept
Your artworks The images the app made for you, as files, and the index that lists them. Your device only
Tags and favourites The words you attached to an artwork, and which ones you starred. Your device only
Your AI processing choice The date you agreed that a photo may be sent to be rendered, so you are not asked again every time. Your device only
Settings Language, theme, audio and display preferences. Your device only

None of this is uploaded to us. We cannot read it, cannot recover it for you, and are not able to hand it to anyone else, because it never reaches us.

What leaves your device

This is the complete list. Everything not named here stays on your device.

What is sent Who receives it Purpose

An anonymous installation identifier Google Firebase Authentication and Firestore To hold the credit balance for this installation. It is a random identifier, tied to no name, email address or device identifier.

What we hold on our side. One record per installation, under a random identifier the app is given when it first runs: the number of credits that installation has left, and whether its starter credits were granted. There is no name, no email address, no device identifier, and no history of what you made attached to it. It is kept until you ask us to remove it — write to idatagear@gmail.com with nothing more than a request to delete, and we will remove the record. Uninstalling the app does not remove it on its own, because nothing in the record identifies you well enough for us to find it without being told.

Separately from the list above, anything you deliberately send — through the system share sheet, an export, a printout or the clipboard — goes wherever you send it. That is your choice each time, and it is not covered by the table above.

What the app does not do

For as long as this policy is the current one:

  • There is no sign-up, no email address, no password and no profile. The app signs in anonymously so that a credit balance can exist, and that identifier is all it is.
  • We do not store your photo or your artwork on a server. The photo passes through to be rendered; the artwork comes straight back to your device.
  • Your photos are not used to train any model of ours, and we do not ask Google to use them for training.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • This version contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • The app does not ask for or read your location.
  • The app does not record audio and requests no microphone access at runtime.
  • The app does not read your contacts, calendar, messages, call log or files outside its own storage.
