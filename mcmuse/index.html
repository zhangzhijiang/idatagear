How it works

From photo to artwork in four taps.

Pick a photo

  1. On Home, tap Camera to take a new one or Photos to choose from your library.
  2. You can replace or remove it before you go on.

Pick your styles

  1. In Create, tick as many styles as you like.
  2. Tap a style's thumbnail to see its example and what it does.
  3. Or start from a style on Home: its button opens Create with that style already chosen.

Choose a size

  1. Pick 1K, 2K or 4K. That is 1, 2 or 4 credits per artwork.
  2. The bar at the bottom spells it out, for example 3 styles selected · 4K · 12 credits, before you press Create.

Save, share, try again

  1. McMuse works through the styles four at a time and shows one card per style.
  2. Each card has Save and Share.
  3. Try Another Style keeps the photo; Create Another starts fresh.

What happens to your photo

Clear about where your photo goes.

McMuse asks before your first artwork and names Google's Gemini as the service that does the rendering. Nothing is sent until you agree.

Location and every other EXIF tag are stripped from the photo before it leaves the device.

McMuse does not keep your photo or the artwork on a server. The artwork comes straight back to your phone.

You do not need an account. Signing in with Google is optional, and only lets your credits follow you to a new phone.

Google's safety filters screen photos and results. If one is refused, no credits are spent, and anything the AI makes can be reported from inside the app.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between the art styles and the 35 more styles?

The 16 art styles repaint your photo in a medium, such as oil, watercolour, pencil or Chinese ink wash. The 35 more styles reimagine it as something else, such as a pin collection, a brick build, a film strip or an Ukiyo-e woodblock print.

Can I make several styles from one photo?

Yes. Tick as many of the 16 art styles as you like for the same photo. McMuse works through them four at a time and shows one card per style, so you can compare them side by side.

What sizes can McMuse make?

1K, 2K or 4K, chosen before you start. An artwork uses 1 credit at 1K, 2 at 2K and 4 at 4K, and the total is written on the button before you press it. The size is recorded on the finished piece.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google is optional and only lets your credits follow you to a new phone.

What happens to my photo?

McMuse asks before the first artwork and names Google's Gemini as the service that renders it. Location and every other EXIF tag are stripped before the photo leaves the device, and neither the photo nor the artwork is kept on a server: the artwork comes straight back to your phone.

Where are my artworks kept?

In the gallery on your phone, not in a cloud account. You can filter it by style, by day, week, month or year, and by tag, and save or share any artwork.

Which languages does McMuse support?

English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. It follows your device, or you can choose in Settings.
